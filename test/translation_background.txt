Game world and mechanics
Timberborn is a settlement simulation about beavers building and maintaining a colony. They construct buildings, grow food and trees, process goods, manage water, and transport resources. The setting uses beaver-themed humor, woodworking, engineering, and occasional machinery. Do not interpret its buildings as military equipment just because an English word also has a military meaning. A storage tank holds liquid; a water wheel generates mechanical power; a shaft transmits that power. A dam, levee, floodgate, sluice, pump, water source, and reservoir are distinct structures with different functions. Preserve those distinctions when choosing the target-language terms.

Beavers need drinking water and food. Water management involves river flow, stored water, droughts, flooding, and toxic badwater. Drought is a season or event affecting the available water, not a general metaphor for scarcity. Badwater is a game-specific contaminated liquid and should remain distinct from ordinary water or merely muddy water.
